Why teams switch

Fewer meetings, more shipped work

Every part of Nexus is built to remove a step. The result is a tool that gets out of the way so the work stays visible.

Keyboard-first

Every command is one keystroke away. Jump between issues, boards, and filters without leaving the keyboard.

Real-time sync

Comments, assignees, and status update instantly across the team, with a full offline fallback for flights and commutes.

Cycles that keep cadence

Set a cycle length, plan the work, and watch velocity chart itself. No spreadsheet, no stand-up recap.

Cycle analytics

Know your velocity without a meeting

Nexus rolls each cycle into a clean chart: planned against delivered, so the stand-up starts with facts instead of guesses.

  • Velocity and total per cycle, computed from your data
