# Photo Abstract — 抽象记忆面板

The **photo-abstract path**: the third zine path for photographic / poetic briefs. The photo stays as the **principal section** (upper/main) and a **derived abstract memory panel** reconstructs its observed spatial / tonal / color relations as a sparse motif below it, with one poetic English title (2–5 words) on the panel. Route at the zine-ui SKILL.md §0; the compact rules live there; this file is the depth.

> **Origin & license.** Distilled from `photo-abstract-editorial` (a Codex skill, downloaded 2026-08-09; source repo declares no license — treat as reference material and keep attribution where feasible). Not covered by finesse-ui's MIT terms for redistribution (zine-ui carries its own LICENSE). Inherits the substrate (universal craft floor), the cheapness blacklist, and the a11y gates — see the *Inherited constraints* section of the zine-ui SKILL.md.
>
> **Relation to the other two paths:** 实景拼贴 keeps the photo and extends outward from it; 影像蒸馏 removes the photo and rebuilds from emotion; **photo-abstract keeps the photo AND distills its relations into a dedicated panel** — the diptych. The panel is not a thumbnail, not a trace, not a posterized photo, not a style transfer.

## 0. Method chain

`DECONSTRUCT → SELECTIVE PRESERVATION → ABSTRACT/DISTILL → RECONSTRUCT`

1. **Deconstruct** — identify 3–6 decisive spatial facts: subject relations, scale, axes, direction, intervals, overlap, depth, rhythm, light, color roles, negative space.
2. **Selective preservation** — which facts come from *subject mass* vs *structure axes / repetition / asymmetry / color hierarchy*.
3. **Abstract / distill** — delete surface texture, perspective detail, background noise, low-information decoration.
4. **Reconstruct** — reorganize the kept relations with the minimum number of marks.
5. **Acceptance test** — first glance is a minimal abstract composition; second glance is *this specific photo*. If it reads as a generic icon set or a posterized photo at either glance, it fails.

## 1. The photo's dual role

- **Principal section** — faithful display (upper/main). Proportional scaling or a slight crop for the composition seam only. **Never** redraw, extend, replace, retouch, filter, or filter-look.
- **Panel source** — the panel is derived *only* from the photo's observed relations. No external images, scenes, objects, colors, or symbols.

## 2. Abstraction grading (subject-adaptive — "minimum necessary recognition")

| Subject | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Ordinary scenes / nature / light / horizons / water / crowds | Direction, density, interval, hierarchy, motion, color relations only — no full outlines |
| Landmark architecture / distinctive shapes | 1–3 identity cues max (outline, negative space, eaves line, taper, arch, spire, stacked rhythm); no windows, brickwork, carving, railing detail |
| Organic clusters (balloons, canopies, clouds, lights) | Overlapping soft organic blocks expressing density / rise / spread; no interior pattern, no literal highlights |
| Crowds | One continuous irregular short vertical mark or gently tapered block per person (head + body one unit); no heads, limbs, faces, clothes |
| Railings / roads / horizons / shores | Compress to 1–2 fine horizontal axes with a few irregular breaks |
| Small objects (bells, lanterns) | 2–3 flat marks; identity via proportion and position, not volume or material |

## 3. Mark system

- **1 primary family** — flat/micro-organic blocks · soft circles or irregular masses · arc/taper strokes · short bars / stacked bands · simplified architectural mass.
- **≤2 supporting families** — fine lines / structural axes · short vertical bars / isolated dots / micro outlines · restrained figure marks · sparse repetition.
- Every mark must trace to a fact in the source photo. No invented decoration, symmetry, pattern, color, or object added "to look good".
- Avoid ruled spacing: adjacent marks carry slight scale / position / pause differences — observed rhythm, not an infographic or vector icon set.

## 4. Adaptive proportion (never a mechanical split)

| Photo orientation | Photo section | Panel |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal / strong horizontal spread | 38%–52% | 48%–62% |
| Tall / architecture / figures | 55%–68% | 32%–45% |
| Near-square / balanced weight | 48%–58% | 42%–52% |

- ±8% latitude; overall harmony wins. Canvas ratio follows the photo, not a fixed 1:1 / 3:4 grid.
- Seam: clean, direct, shadowless flat join. No torn edges, frame, shadow, tape, collage, or mockup effect.

## 5. Panel layout

- Ground: completely uniform, continuous ivory `#F3F0E8` family (or a tonal sibling in harmony with the motif). No gradient, texture, grain, vignette, stains, or scan artifacts — the panel is a clean plane.
- Motif: 30%–42% of panel width · ≤28%–34% of panel height · 65%–80% clean whitespace.
- Adjustments: fine horizontals (horizons/bridges/crowds) may span 45%–68% width but stay low; tall subjects (towers) may gain height but never fill the panel; compact clusters stay gathered.
- Motif-internal relations (scale, direction, spacing, overlap, repetition, weight, asymmetry) must survive as a whole — never shrink to a generic icon to satisfy a percentage.

## 6. Color system (only from the photo — desaturate, reduce)

- 1 main color role · 1 dark structure role · 1 light / neutral role · **≤2 small accent roles**.
- Accents must exist in the photo as important colors and appear only on a few key marks.
- No neon, no invented complementaries, no competing accents.

> Web note: this is the inherited color lock applied in the zine register — the panel's palette is sampled from the photo, not chosen from a palette library.

## 7. Title (English, 2–5 words, 信达雅)

- **信** — corresponds to a real subject relation or visual fact in the photo.
- **达** — plain, natural, comprehensible; no stiff translation.
- **雅** — poetic with resonance; no melodrama, obscurity, or forced depth.
- Naming directions (pick one): light/time entering a space · a relation/dialogue between two subjects · something brief appearing/floating/receding/pausing · a metaphor drawn from color, structural axes, or motion · an original compound + short gloss.
- Forbidden: travelogue titles, place-name intros, photo jargon, empty "Memory / Dream / Moment", grand narratives unrelated to photo facts.
- Default: one title. Subtitle (3–7 words) only when it adds a semantic layer; rendered once each. Decide the final title internally, then render it exactly — no candidate lists, no commentary.

## 8. Title typography

- Placement: panel only — bottom-left (motif off-center right / horizontal compositions) or bottom-centered (motif centered, clear axis, symmetric architecture). **Never** on the photo, inside the motif, bottom-right, or at the canvas edge. ~6%–9% safe margin from left/bottom edges.
- Faces: restrained editorial serif; architectural/structural subjects → sturdy book serif; light/nature/lyrical subjects → humanist fine serif; subtitle → smaller elegant italic serif. No commercial bold, no sans ad face, no cartoon/handwriting/decorative fonts.
- Color: a darker restrained hue drawn from the photo or motif (deep blue-grey, dark green, wine, deep purple, charcoal). Readable on the ivory ground; **not** pure black by default; never the brightest accent.

## 9. CLEAN mode (atmosphere sources)

Atmosphere comes only from: whitespace, distance, pause, asymmetry, scale difference, limited marks, restrained color.

Panel ground bans: gradients, light shifts, shadows, glow, vignettes, banding, seams, paper texture, grain, noise, fiber, watercolor bleed, fog, stains, fading, grey haze, scan marks, stickers, compression artifacts. Marks may carry slight natural edges and hand-irregularity, but never rely on material noise for atmosphere.

## 10. Web translation

The diptych maps to a one-page or single-section build:

- **Photo block** — the real photo (per `asset-sourcing.md`: real imagery, not placeholders) occupies the principal band; `object-fit` with the §4 ratio locked on the band; no filters, no duotone overlays, no hard crops beyond §4 latitude.
- **Abstract panel section** — ivory ground (`#F3F0E8` family); the motif is a **hand-built SVG/CSS composition** derived per §2–§3 (never an inline random icon set, never an emoji, never clip-art). Mark family discipline applies to the SVG markup itself.
- **Title** — editorial serif display, §8 placement/color; rendered once.
- The seam is a hairline translucent border (craft floor), flat — no shadow/tape/frame effects.
- It is still a *page*: keeps nav, links, scroll (a zine that forgets it's a page is the register's anti-cheap failure).
- Runs the inherited blacklist + pre-flight + this file's pre-flight (§12).

## 11. Photo-abstract anti-cheap additions (in addition to the inherited blacklist)

- Photo redrawn / filtered / hard-cropped → the photo stays faithful (scale + slight crop only).
- Panel reads as a thumbnail / silhouette trace / posterized photo / vector icon set → it must be *relations*, not outlines.
- Motif marks with no source fact → every mark traces to the photo.
- Mechanical 50/50 split → adaptive proportion per §4.
- Invented decoration / colors / symmetry "for balance" → from the photo only, restrained.
- Unreadable "poetic" title → the inherited WCAG AA floor still applies; the ivory may be pale, the type may not be.
- More than 1 primary mark family → 1 + ≤2 supporting, max.
- Extra text on the piece (dates, captions, color chips, legends) → title only.

## 12. Photo-abstract pre-flight (in addition to the inherited blacklist + pre-flight)

- [ ] Photo faithful: scale/crop only — no redraw, filter, extension.
- [ ] 3–6 spatial facts named; the motif's keep/compress/discard is explicit.
- [ ] Panel derived solely from photo relations; every mark traceable to a fact.
- [ ] 1 primary mark family + ≤2 supporting; no invented decoration.
- [ ] Adaptive proportion per §4 (not 50/50); clean flat seam; canvas follows the photo.
- [ ] Ivory uniform ground; CLEAN mode — zero texture/gradient/grain in the panel.
- [ ] Color roles ≤ 1 main + 1 dark + 1 light + ≤2 accents, all sampled from the photo.
- [ ] One title, grounded, editorial serif, panel only; no extra text anywhere.
- [ ] Still passes the substrate + §8 blacklist + §9 a11y gates.

## 13. Dials

SOUL 8–10 (the register is a strong persona) · SPECTACLE 2–4 (the diptych + motif craft *is* the spectacle — no hero engine) · DENSITY 3–6 (the panel breathes; the photo carries the density).
